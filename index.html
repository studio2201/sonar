True stereo separation, dual transparency controls, and 1-click suite color sync.

True Left & Right Stereo FFT

Captures @DEFAULT_MONITOR@ in-process via gi://Gst with 16-band energy averaging per channel and falling 8-bit peak-hold caps.

Transparency & Suite Borders

Independent Background Opacity (0–100%) and 8-Bit Brick Opacity (20–100%), rounded outer corner arc, and 1-click Studio 2201 color sync.
